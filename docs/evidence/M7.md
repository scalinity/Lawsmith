## Architecture

`RunCoordinator` owns a retained authoring/replay source and one comparison. `Comparison` owns one defensive private complete fork, its original frozen semantic root, one B controller/host, immutable committed trace chunks, a complete baseline endpoint, and at most one yielding calculation world. Source recording tails are not inputs to either future. Baseline samples store every living body's actual seven-component pose and stable identity at each tick. Extensions continue from the complete endpoint and preserve earlier chunks. Consumer buffers are copies.

B has its own 64-entry/2 MiB bounded undo history and a 4 MiB append-only consumed-command suffix. Replay Alternate restores the original fork and consumes that suffix at exact addresses, retaining local undo at its end. New Alternate clears both. Only B advances during display frames. Ghost outlines are pure render geometry; solid B and outlined A remain distinguished when overlapping.

The 64 MiB comparison admission accounts for actual typed-array byte lengths and conservative object/string sizes, retained fork/endpoint, identity indexes, trace chunks, suffix/history reservations, controller/host/render buffers, and checkpoint/export/observer scratch. Ordinary M6B checkpoints keep their separate 64 MiB budget. Refusal leaves the old horizon intact.

Export Alternate Setup writes a normal tick-zero scene from source authored body/emitter definitions and B's current fields/ambient settings. It does not bind/clean the main scene or retire recovery. Guard cancellation retains the comparison; accepted replacement disposes it.
