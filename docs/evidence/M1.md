`src/rendering/backend.ts` and `src-tauri/` are unchanged: Rust stays thin, and native focus comes from the pinned `onFocusChanged` JS API under the existing `core:default` capability. New tokens in `src/style.css`: `--floor`, `--grid-minor`, `--body`, `--danger`.

Choices worth knowing:
- The floor carries the sphere's material (friction 0.6, restitution 0.25), so Rapier's default average combine yields exactly the recipe's contact values. SPEC §2.2 does not name the floor's material.
- A gesture ends on its last valid value. A pointer that reports `buttons === 0` while a drag holds it ends that drag as a release would have (gizmo: commit; orbit: its own `pointercancel` path). Escape, `pointercancel`, a `lostpointercapture` that leaves the drag running, focus loss and a gap cancel instead, restoring the gesture's starting value through a new `putField`.
- The window-drag element over the top 40 px stays the same native `data-tauri-drag-region`. It sets `pointer-events: none` while the law or a gizmo handle lies under the pointer, and for the duration of any gesture. Once a gesture or an orbit ends, or the window regains focus, the next frame recomputes this from the last pointer position, after the restored law value is applied.
- OrbitControls has no public way to stop inertia, and its `update()` applies residual damping even while disabled. When a gizmo drag begins, `viewport.ts` clears the two fields `update()` itself zeroes when damping is off (pinned three 0.186.1); startup fails loudly if they are missing.
- Arrows sample the host's own `CompiledField` on a 5×5×5 lattice at local fractions ±0.875/±0.4375/0. The outer layer sits at d = 0.875, where f = 0.25 gives weight 0.5. Scale: 0.05 m per m/s², hidden below 0.05 m/s². Refresh: on each new applied value, at most 30 Hz during a gesture.
