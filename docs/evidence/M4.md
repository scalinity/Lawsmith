## The retained transition (SPEC §9.1, §12; AC2)

`SimulationHost.step()` already holds, for each body, the start-of-step center and velocity, each law's `(A_i, K_i)`, the aggregate, β, λ and the force. For the one explained body it copies those values into a fixed buffer while the force path computes them. The authoritative arithmetic and its order are unchanged: the law loop became an indexed loop over the same array, and the only additions are copies and a velocity getter. After `world.step()` it builds the observation:

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `bodyId`, `fromTick = n`, `toTick = n+1` | Which body, which transition |
| `cursor`, `laws` | The command cursor after boundary n settled, and the frozen law definitions applied (the readout says "laws as loaded" or "after N applied edits") |
| `center`, `velocity`, `mass` | The body's state at n as the step read it, and the engine-reported mass |
| `drive` A = g + ΣA_i, `drag` K = ΣK_i, `beta`, `lambda`, `maxApplied` | The aggregate and the shared factors |
| `instantaneous` A − Kv, `submitted` λ·a*, `force` | Before β and λ; the submitted acceleration; the force exactly as added |
| `contributions[i]`: `drive` A_i, `drag` K_i, `applied` λβ(A_i − K_i v) | Each law's share, in stable law order |
| `gravityApplied` λβg | Gravity's share |
| `after`, `contacts` | The center and velocity at n+1; the stable IDs of the colliders whose contact carried a normal impulse in the step, sorted |

The observation is outside the canonical state, the engine snapshot, the run root, the scene file and command scheduling. Choosing another body leaves the current observation in place under its own `bodyId`, so it never reads as the new body's; the readout says "No step recorded for this body yet" until that body completes a step. A reset clears it.

**Paused preview.** `previewTransition()` runs the same kernel and adapter from the explained body's current center and velocity under the applied laws and writes nothing. The readout labels it "Next step … if nothing changes first"; the last step stays a separate view and is never overwritten.

**Contacts.** After the step, `world.contactPairsWith` and `world.contactPair` list the explained body's manifolds; a partner is named when `contactImpulse` summed over its points is positive. A probe against the pinned engine established both facts this relies on: the impulse read after the step is that step's (a 1 kg ball's 29→30 rebound impulse, 3.67018 N·s, equals its unexplained Δv exactly, and it is zero in free flight), and querying leaves the snapshot byte-identical to an unqueried twin. Proximity pairs with zero impulse are common, so pair existence is not reported as contact. No contact acceleration is derived: the readout names the partners and shows the observed velocity after the step, as SPEC §12 provides.

## Findings

1. **An M4 row pushed accepted M2 fields below the panel's fold** (fixed). The first candidate (`e300f8e`) put an "Arrows for" row in the Laws section. At the built-in display's height with two laws that moved the selected law's Position fields below the Scene panel's visible area, and `m2-authoring`'s precise-value click landed on the canvas, deselecting the law, so its rejection step never ran. `3b29c69` moves the filter beside the probe and trail switches above the transport; the Laws and details markup is again M3's. Every native scenario then ran again on `3b29c69`, and `m2-authoring` passed all 20 checks. The panel's general height stays with M8 (M2 finding 8); M4 adds nothing above the details.
2. **Contact naming depends on the pinned engine's narrow phase.** After `world.step()` a manifold's `contactImpulse` is that step's impulse, and pairs within the prediction distance with zero impulse are common; both were established against Rapier 0.21.0 before the design relied on them, and the contact fixtures pin them. A Rapier upgrade must re-run `tests/explanation.test.ts`. Informational.
3. **Overlay state survives a scene load.** Probe, trail and filter settings are session visualization state, not scene data, so they carry across Open. `m4-perf` first assumed probes started off in its legibility segment and toggled them the wrong way; the check now lives in `m4-legible`, which sets each switch only when the app reports a different state. Informational.
4. **Probes are born throughout the box around the enabled laws' supports**, including space below the floor; those probes are hidden by the opaque floor and pass through it, as collision-free probes must. Informational.
5. **32-body trails go to the oldest living bodies**, and the explained body always gets a slot (evicting the shortest trail if all are taken). Where bodies have short lifetimes the trails are short: on Collisions at tick 1200 the 32 trails hold 800 samples. Informational.
6. **Native QA shared the screen.** The first evidence chain yielded to outside input at 18:49:48 and was stopped; a Notification Center window covered the screen for more than 10 s during the visual pass and the guard refused input; focus left the app for 1.1 s during a P2 run, which the protocol invalidated; and one synthetic Play click did not register in `m3-demo` (M0 finding 1). Each affected scenario ran again in full. Informational.
7. **Synthetic drags reach WebKit only in part** (M2 finding 3): the coefficient-gauge drag committed through the stale-buttons reconciliation. Informational.
8. **Old trails stayed drawn after a paused Reset or Open** (review finding 1, high; fixed). The recorder cleared only inside `record()`, which runs after a completed step, and paused frames synced probes but never trails. In the review's reproduction (32-body trails on Falling Stream, advanced to tick 100), a paused Reset left 13 old trails and 312 vertices drawn at tick 0, and a paused Open of an empty scene left them over a world with no bodies.
