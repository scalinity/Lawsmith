## Corrections settled from the contracts

1. **P3 setup order (SPEC §18.2).** The previous capture computed the 7,200-tick baseline before warming. That was an implementation gap, and it is now corrected.
   - Every P3 capture is its own comparison, in SPEC §18.2's order:
     1. Compare from here forks and builds A's default 600-tick baseline, measured on its own.
     2. Shift+P warms a disposable continuation of the fork for 10 s. When it reaches A's 600-tick horizon it is restored from the fork and plays on, a deliberate pause outside any sample.
     3. The fork is restored, with no intervention left.
     4. The full 7,200-tick baseline is precomputed.
     5. 60 s of active playback are measured.
     6. Close comparison ends the capture.
   - Every phase is logged: the `p0` phases warmup, restore, precompute and measure, and the `comparison` events fork, baseline-start, baseline-committed and closed. `m9perf.py` checks the order, that the 600-tick construction lies between fork and warmup, that the 7,200-tick job lies between restore and measure, that no baseline work happens inside the measured minute, and that the three forks have equal state (tick, cursor, engine hash).
   - Source state is untouched: each fork is taken from the same paused source, and setup stays off-sample.
   - All three captures keep 10 s of warmup and 60 s of measurement.
   - No contract contradiction was found.
